### 丝绸之路


欢迎各位参加丝绸之路游。我相信为期两周的游览将成为您一生中最难忘的经历之一。
丝绸之路的历史可以追溯到公元前2世纪。当时一名中国官员，朝廷的使者张骞沿着这条连接亚欧两大洲的贸易通道出使西域。这条通道源于长安城及今日的西安，一路穿越陕西省甘肃省境内的河西走廊，新疆的塔里木盆地、帕米尔山区、阿富汗、伊朗、伊拉克以及叙利亚最后抵达地中海东岸，全程7000公里。其中4000多公里的路段位于中国境内。
15世纪前的1000多年里，中国通过这条通道给西域各国带去了丝绸、织物、火药、造纸术和印刷术。同时这条通道也从国外给我们引进了佛教、伊斯兰教以及葡萄、核桃、石榴、黄瓜、玻璃、香水等产品。因为中国丝绸是沿着这条道进入西方国家的，所以欧洲学者将此道称作丝绸之路。

Welcome everyone to the Silk Road tour. I believe this two-week trip will be one of the most memorable experiences of your life.

The history of the Silk Road dates back to the 2nd century BC. At that time, a Chinese official named Zhang Qian traveled to the Western Regions. He followed this trade route that connected Asia and Europe.

The route starts in Chang'an, known today as Xi'an. It crosses the Hexi Corridor in Shaanxi and Gansu, the Tarim Basin in Xinjiang, and the Pamir Mountains. It continues through Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq, and Syria, finally reaching the eastern coast of the Mediterranean Sea. The total distance is 7,000 kilometers. More than 4,000 kilometers of this route are inside China.

For over 1,000 years before the 15th century, China used this route to bring silk, fabrics, gunpowder, paper-making, and printing to the West. At the same time, this route brought Buddhism, Islam, and products like grapes, walnuts, pomegranates, cucumbers, glass, and perfume into China.

Because Chinese silk traveled along this road to reach Western countries, European scholars named it the "Silk Road."

### 香港中文大学

我代表香港中文大学，欢迎各位同学。香港中文大学简称中大成立于1963。
由当时的崇基学院、新亚书院和联合书院合并而成，中大是一所研究型综合大学，以结合传统与现代融汇中国与西方为创校使命中大设有文学院、工商管理学院、教育学院、工程学院、医学院、理学院、社会科学院和法学院等8个学院。
40多年来，我们一直致力于弘扬中华传统文化，坚持双语教育，并推行独特的书院制度。在香港高等教育界卓然而立。
中大校园占地达134公顷，是世界上最美丽的校园之一。中大的师生来自世界各地，我们有教职员5200多人，近万名本科生，约2000名研究生，其中约2500人来自45个不同的国家和地区。我们开设的主副修课程各有五十八门，可供学生自由选修。

On behalf of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, I would like to welcome all of you. Founded in 1963, The Chinese University of Hong Kong is often referred to as CUHK.

It was formed by the merger of three existing colleges: Chung Chi College, New Asia College, and United College. CUHK is a comprehensive research university with a founding mission to combine tradition with modernity and to bring together China and the West. The university has eight faculties, including Arts, Business Administration, Education, Engineering, Medicine, Science, Social Science, and Law.

For over 40 years, we have been dedicated to promoting traditional Chinese culture. We are committed to bilingual education and maintain a unique collegiate system. These features make us stand out in Hong Kong's higher education sector.

The CUHK campus covers 134 hectares and is recognized as one of the most beautiful campuses in the world. Our faculty and students come from all over the globe. We have over 5,200 staff members, nearly 10,000 undergraduate students, and about 2,000 postgraduate students. Among them, around 2,500 come from 45 different countries and regions. We offer 58 major and 58 minor programs, giving students the freedom to choose their path of study.
